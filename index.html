Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in weekly meal preparation?

Standard weekly prep covers 4 entrée protein selections, 5 side dishes, prepared salad ingredients, 1 dessert, and 3 breakfast selections. All items are cooked in your kitchen, portioned, labeled, and accompanied by heating instructions.

Are groceries included in the published service price?

No. Service rates ($250 for up to 4 people; $300 for 6-8 people) cover culinary labor, menu design, prep time, and kitchen sanitization. Groceries are billed separately at direct supermarket receipt cost with zero markup.

Can dietary restrictions and food allergies be accommodated?

Absolutely. We routinely accommodate Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Keto, Low Sodium, Paleo, Vegetarian, Vegan, and specific severe food allergies.

What geographic areas in South Carolina are served?

We serve Beaufort, Port Royal, Bluffton, Hilton Head Island, Dataw Island, Fripp Island, and the surrounding Lowcountry communities.

Do I need to be home while the chef is cooking?

No. Many clients provide keyless entry or garage access codes. Chef Josh arrives, cooks, packages the food in your refrigerator, leaves your kitchen spotless, and locks up.
